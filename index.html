💡 How to Play

1

Tap to Flap — Tap anywhere to make Modi fly. Don't stop tapping!

2

Avoid Obstacles — Dodge pipes, watch out for cockroaches 🪳.

3

Score Points — Pass through pipe gaps to earn score & coins. That's how you win!

4

Use Hits — Hits protect you once from a cockroach collision. Buy more in the Store.

5

Collect Melodies — Catch melodies mid-flight. They let you respawn after a crash.

6

Buy Melody Pack — Get melodies from the Store & spin the roulette for extra melodies.

7

Customize — Unlock new Themes 🎨 and Vehicles ✈️ with your coins!
